AC Gas Filling & Leak Repair

Low refrigerant gas is the most frequent reason an AC stops cooling. NCR Cooling Centre uses high-accuracy manifold pressure gauges and digital instruments to identify microscopic copper tube leaks, repair flare joints, pull a deep vacuum, and refill pure 100% genuine refrigerant gas (R-32, R-410A, R-22) for maximum cooling efficiency.

What's Included in Our Service:

High-Pressure Nitrogen Leak Testing
Brazing & Copper Pipe Joint Leak Seal
Vacuum Pump Evacuation to remove moisture
Digital Manifold Gauge Pressure Calibration
Genuine R32, R410A, and R22 Gas Cylinders
Sub-cooling & Superheat Temperature Verification

Common Issues & How We Resolve Them:

1. Ice Formation on AC Copper PipesIndicates severe refrigerant shortage; we locate the leak source, braze it, and refill full gas.
2. AC Running but Room Stays WarmWe test running suction pressure with a manifold gauge to verify exact gas level deficiency.
3. Hissing Sound Near Outdoor UnitSign of active gas leak at service valve or condenser bend; needs prompt leak brazing.

Our Step-by-Step Service Procedure:

  1. 1Connect Manifold Pressure Gauge to Service Port
  2. 2Perform Nitrogen Pressure & Soap Leak Detection
  3. 3Brazing & Tightening Flare Nuts
  4. 4Deep Vacuumizing with Rotary Vacuum Pump
  5. 5Weight-Based Gas Charging as per Company Specs

Frequently Asked Questions (AC Gas Filling & Leak Repair):

Q: Why is vacuuming necessary before gas filling?

Vacuuming removes air and moisture from inside the copper coils. Without vacuuming, moisture reacts with oil and burns out the compressor.

Q: How do I know if my AC needs gas refilling?

If your AC compressor is running but air from vents is not chilled, or if thin copper pipes have white frost, gas is deficient.

Q: Is it dangerous if a geyser trips the MCB?

Yes! An MCB trip indicates electrical current is leaking into the water. Turn off the main switch immediately and call our technicians.

Q: Why is hard water damaging geysers in Gurgaon?

Gurgaon groundwater has high TDS and calcium content which coats the heating coil, causing it to overheat and burn out quickly. Routine descaling prevents this.
